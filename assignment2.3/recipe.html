How to Prepare Akara

Akara is a popular Nigerian food made from beans. It is usually eaten with bread or pap. Below are the ingredients and steps needed to prepare it.

Ingredients

  • Beans
  • Onion
  • Pepper
  • Salt
  • Vegetable oil
  • Water

Steps to Prepare Akara

  1. Wash the beans and remove the skin.
  2. Blend the beans with a little water.
  3. Cut the onion and pepper.
  4. Add them to the blended beans.
  5. Add salt and mix everything together.
  6. Heat oil in a frying pan.
  7. Scoop the mixture into the hot oil.
  8. Fry until it becomes golden brown.
  9. Remove from the oil and allow it to cool.

Safety Notes

Warning: Be careful when frying because hot oil can burn your skin.

Do not pour water into hot oil.

Cooking Terms

Blending
Mixing the beans until they become smooth.
Frying
Cooking food in hot oil.
Draining
Removing excess oil from food.

Serving

Serve the akara with bread, pap or custard.
